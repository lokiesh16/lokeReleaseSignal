The stated goal was simply to add a breakfast food item, yet 11 interactions were required to land on this screen showing one egg already logged. A typical add-food flow should take 3-5 steps: open Food Log → select Breakfast tab → tap Add Food → search/select item → confirm. Steps 1-4 appear to involve navigating to the right screen, steps 5-8 involve redundant or repeated taps in very close proximity (steps 7 and 8 are nearly identical coordinates, suggesting a mis-tap was repeated), and steps 9-11 seem to confirm or finalize the entry. The near-duplicate taps at steps 7-8 and 10-11 suggest either UI lag causing double-taps or unclear confirmation affordances. The app should provide clearer tap feedback (button state change) to prevent repeated taps, and the path to add a breakfast food should be consolidated.
Judged against: “I am gonna add food - breakfast”
The Food Log screen is clean and readable at a glance, with a clear card layout for the logged egg entry and prominent bottom action buttons. However, the large empty gray area below the single food card wastes screen real estate and makes the UI feel incomplete. The macro icons (wheat for carbs, cheese for fat, drumstick for protein) are unconventional and add cognitive load. The bottom summary circles are small and partially clipped, and the overall flow to add a breakfast item took 11 interactions which is excessive for what should be a straightforward task.
Issues & suggestions
high Usability
Massive empty gray area between the egg card and the bottom buttons makes the screen feel broken or still loading. A user might think content failed to load or wonder where to tap to add more food.
Fix: Either show an empty-state prompt ('Add more foods to Breakfast') with an inline + button, or collapse the dead space so the bottom action bar sits closer to the content.
medium Accessibility
The four bottom summary circles (Cals, Carbs, Fat, Protein) are very small — roughly 60px diameter — and the arc/progress indicator inside them is barely visible. The numbers inside are legible but the context of what the arc means is unclear without color labels, and the arc for 'Cals' and 'Protein' appears nearly empty making it hard to distinguish from 'Carbs' which shows 0.
Fix: Increase circle size or replace with a horizontal progress bar row with explicit labels and values, ensuring touch targets and visual clarity meet at least 44pt guidelines.
medium Consistency
The macro icons on the food card (orange wheat for Carbs, yellow cheese wedge for Fat, purple drumstick for Protein) don't match the abstract circular arc style used in the bottom summary — same data, completely different visual language.
Fix: Use the same icon set or color coding for macros both on the food card and in the bottom summary circles so users immediately recognize they represent the same nutrients.
medium Usability
The pie-chart icon next to the edit and delete buttons on the egg card has no label and its function is ambiguous — it's unclear whether it shows nutritional breakdown, serving size history, or something else.
Fix: Add a short tooltip or label on first use, or replace with a more universally understood icon (e.g., an 'info' i icon) and ensure its action is clearly distinct from edit/delete.
low Visual design
The 'Back' text navigation in the top-left uses a small chevron + text, while the date navigator below uses separate left/right chevrons with a dropdown. Two different navigation patterns on the same screen creates mild visual inconsistency.
Fix: Standardize navigation affordances — if using icon+text for back, consider the same pattern for date navigation, or at minimum ensure chevron sizes and weights match.
